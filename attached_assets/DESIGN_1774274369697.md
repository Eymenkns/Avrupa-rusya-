# Design System Documentation: Chapterlog Logistics

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star: "The Obsidian Monolith"
This design system is built upon the concept of **The Obsidian Monolith**. In the world of high-end logistics, 'Chapterlog' represents an immovable, authoritative force—a digital fortress of reliability. We reject the "friendly" aesthetics of modern SaaS in favor of a **Grimdark Modern** philosophy. This is Industrial Gothic: a marriage of brutalist architecture and editorial precision.

The experience is defined by **intentional severity**. By utilizing 0px radiuses (sharp corners) and a high-contrast color palette of metallic charcoal and burnt orange, we create an atmosphere of power. We break the standard "web template" look through **Extreme Asymmetry**—where large-scale futuristic typography overlaps dark, textured surfaces—and **Cinematic Depth**, treating the screen as a series of heavy, physical plates layered in deep space.

---

## 2. Colors & Surface Philosophy
The palette is rooted in the "Grimdark" aesthetic: deep, atmospheric shadows punctuated by the heat of industrial forge-fire.

### The Palette
- **Background (`#131313`):** The void. All interfaces begin here.
- **Primary (`#ffb5a0` to `#fb5b2d`):** The "Burnt Orange." Use this sparingly for high-impact calls to action and critical status indicators.
- **Surface Tiers:** 
    - `surface_container_lowest`: `#0e0e0e` (Used for deep background wells)
    - `surface_container_low`: `#1c1b1b` (Standard section background)
    - `surface_container_high`: `#2a2a2a` (Elevated interactive cards)

### The "No-Line" Rule
Standard 1px borders are strictly prohibited for sectioning. Structural definition must be achieved through **background color shifts**. To separate a sidebar from a main feed, transition from `surface` to `surface_container_low`. If an element requires more focus, utilize `surface_bright` to create a subtle glow rather than a stroke.

### The Glass & Gradient Rule
To prevent the UI from feeling "flat," use **Glassmorphism** for floating menus or navigation overlays. Combine `surface_container_highest` at 70% opacity with a `backdrop-blur` of 20px. 
**Signature Texture:** Apply a linear gradient from `primary` to `primary_container` on high-level buttons to simulate the "heat" of a glowing industrial element.

---

## 3. Typography: The Dual-Era Tension
The typography system creates a "Warhammer-inspired but clean" tension between the future and the ancient.

*   **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** This represents the "Industrial" side. It is a bold, futuristic sans-serif. Use `display-lg` (3.5rem) with tight tracking (-0.05em) to create an authoritative, monolithic look.
*   **Titles & Body (Newsreader):** This represents the "Gothic" side. This subtle serif adds an editorial, high-end feel. It implies history, records, and the "Chapters" of the logistics journey.
*   **Labels (Work Sans):** Functional, high-readability sans-serif for technical data and micro-copy.

**Hierarchy Note:** Always pair a `headline-lg` in Space Grotesk with a `body-md` in Newsreader. The contrast between the aggressive sans-serif and the scholarly serif is the signature of this design system.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth: Tonal Layering
In a world of sharp edges (0px roundedness), depth cannot be communicated through soft "bubbles." We use **Tonal Layering**.

- **The Layering Principle:** Instead of shadows, we stack. A `surface_container_lowest` container sits *inside* a `surface_container_low` section to create a "recessed" look. To create "lift," place a `surface_container_high` element on a `surface` background.
- **Ambient Shadows:** Shadows are reserved for floating elements (modals). They must be massive and faint: `box-shadow: 0 40px 100px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`. Use a tinted shadow color based on the `surface_container_lowest` to maintain atmospheric depth.
- **The "Ghost Border" Fallback:** If a boundary is strictly required for accessibility, use `outline_variant` at **15% opacity**. It should be felt, not seen.

---

## 5. Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Burnt Orange (`primary_container`) with `on_primary_container` text. Sharp 0px corners. Use a subtle inner-glow gradient to simulate metallic depth.
- **Secondary:** Metallic Charcoal (`surface_container_highest`). No border. Text in `on_surface`.
- **States:** On hover, primary buttons should shift to `primary_fixed_dim`, creating a "cooling metal" effect.

### Input Fields
Text inputs use `surface_container_lowest` as a base. The label (`label-md` in Work Sans) should be placed strictly above the field, never inside. The active state is signaled by a `primary` 2px bottom-bar only—avoid full-box outlines.

### Cards & Lists
- **Rule:** Forbid the use of divider lines.
- **Implementation:** Use `spacing-8` (1.75rem) to separate list items. For cards, use a background shift to `surface_container_low`. 
- **The "Logbook" Component:** For logistics tracking, use an editorial layout. Large `display-sm` numbers for dates, paired with Newsreader `body-lg` for descriptions.

### Additional Signature Component: "The Monolith Header"
A full-width, high-contrast header using `surface_container_lowest` that houses a massive `display-lg` title. The title should be partially cropped by the edge of the viewport to emphasize the "power" and scale of the Chapterlog operations.

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- **Embrace the Dark:** Keep 90% of the UI in the `surface` to `surface_container_lowest` range.
- **Use Large Type:** Let the letterforms be the "symbols." A massive 'C' or '01' in Space Grotesk is more powerful than any icon.
- **Maintain Sharpness:** Every corner must be `0px`. Rounding any element destroys the industrial integrity of the system.

### Don't
- **No Divider Lines:** Never use a `1px` line to separate content. Use the spacing scale (`spacing-12` or `spacing-16`) or background shifts.
- **No Vibrant Colors:** Aside from the Burnt Orange (`primary`), avoid blues, greens, or purples. Even "Success" states should be handled with subtle tonal shifts or high-contrast white text.
- **No Standard Shadows:** Avoid small, muddy "drop shadows." If it doesn't look like it's glowing or physically recessed, remove the effect.

### Accessibility Note
While the theme is dark and "grim," ensure all text in `body-md` using Newsreader maintains at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its specific `surface_container` tier. Use `on_surface` or `on_surface_variant` to ensure legibility is never sacrificed for atmosphere.```
